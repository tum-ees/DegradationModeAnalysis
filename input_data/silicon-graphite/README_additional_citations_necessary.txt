Dear user of this tool,

The SiGr blend anode curves shown here were measured from harvested
Molicel P45B electrodes in coin cell half-cell configuration.
They are published alongside this tool (no additional citation needed):

M. Rehm, J. Natterer, J. Eizenhammer, M. Guenthner,
S. Kuecher, C. Korkmaz, F. Roehrer, A. Jossen,
"How to determine the degradation modes of lithium-ion
batteries with silicon-graphite blend electrodes,"
J. Power Sources, vol. 670, 239418, 2026.
DOI: 10.1016/j.jpowsour.2026.239418

In the file names, lithiation and delithiation refer to the anode direction.
For instance, for full-cell charge, choose anode lithiation.
The C in the 0C0x terms at the end of the file names denotes a decimal point.
Thus, 0C03 means 0.03C and 0C02 means 0.02C (dis)charge current.

Available data:
  P45B_Anode_Lithiation_0C02.mat   (0.02C)
  P45B_Anode_Lithiation_0C03.mat   (0.03C)
  P45B_Anode_Delithiation_0C02.mat (0.02C)
  P45B_Anode_Delithiation_0C03.mat (0.03C)
